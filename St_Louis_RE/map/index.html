    How the values were calculated

    The data covers 2010-2019. Pre covers 2010–2015 and post covers 2016–2019, after the Rams' announced their departure in 2016. Percent change divides the difference between period averages by the pre-period average.

    Dollar amounts are nominal. Distance is measured in straight-line miles from the center of a parcel to the coordinates of the stadium.

    Data Sources

    All data came from St. Louis City Assessor's Office. Tax Assessments and Sales Transactions were matched to geographic tax parcels via a city-provided cross-walk.
